# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Terminal clock with a seconds ring, a beating pixel heart and Clawd, who follows the time of day and your
  heart rate through nine moods and five scenes.
- Scenes fade into each other on the minute, and Clawd starts and ends every scene in the same spot.
- Always-on mode that dims the same layout in place, with a cross-fade on Wear OS 6.
- Four accents: Clay, Sky, Mint and Oat.
- Two downloads: one for Wear OS 6 and newer, one for Wear OS 4 and 5.
